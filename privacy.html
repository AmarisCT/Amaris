Privacy

Effective October 19, 2026

This policy covers every website made by amaris ct: this studio site, Artificially Intelligent, and The Weekly Planner. It describes what each one does with information today.

The short version

  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies run on any amaris ct site.
  • amaris ct does not sell or share personal information.
  • Most of what you do on these sites stays in your own browser, on your own device.

What stays in your browser

Some features remember things using your browser's local storage. That information is kept on your device and is not sent to amaris ct.

  • Artificially Intelligent remembers items you save to the Reading Desk and your progress in Learning Institute courses.
  • The Weekly Planner keeps your account and your plans in your browser. See the section below.

You can remove this information at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser settings. Anyone who uses the same browser on the same device may be able to see it.

The Weekly Planner

The Weekly Planner is still in development. Its sign-in is a local feature: the name, email address, and password you enter, along with your plans and theme choices, are saved only in the browser you use. They are not sent to amaris ct or to any server, and they do not follow you to another device or browser.

Because this information lives on your device, please do not use a password that you use anywhere else. If you add a background image by web address, your browser loads that image directly from the site you linked.

Artificially Intelligent

Reading and search

Site search, the Inquiry Engine, and the Lexicon run inside your browser on information already published on the site. Those searches are not sent anywhere.

The Intelligence Index, and the outside-sources search on the home page, look up books, papers, and reference entries for you. When you use them, your search words go directly from your browser to Crossref, Wikipedia, or Open Library, under those services' own privacy policies. amaris ct does not receive or keep them.

Architect sign-in

The Architect login is used only by the site's editors to publish research. It runs on a separate, secured service, and visitors never need to use it.

Visitor contributions

The Research Commons and the Unanswered Archive are designed to accept questions and contributions from visitors. Submissions are currently switched off. Before they open, this page will be updated to explain exactly what is collected, how it is reviewed, and how long it is kept.

Services that help deliver the sites

Like any website, these sites rely on a few outside services. When your browser loads a page, those services receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type.

  • GitHub Pages hosts every amaris ct site. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies some typefaces on Artificially Intelligent and The Weekly Planner. See Google Fonts privacy.
  • Wikimedia Commons supplies the public-domain image of The Creation of Adam on the studio site.

Links to other sites

The sites link to Substack, GitHub, research publishers, and other sources. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and this policy does not cover them.

Changes

When a project starts handling information in a new way, this page is updated first, and the effective date at the top changes.

Questions

For questions about this policy, reach amaris ct through the studio's GitHub profile.
